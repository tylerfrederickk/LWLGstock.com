Key numbers over time.

Snapshots can mislead. Trends don't. Each chart covers every quarter since 2022, taken from the company's 10-Q and 10-K filings. Hover any chart for exact values, or open its table.

Q4 values for burn, net loss and revenue are derived (full-year minus nine months). Share counts come from each filing's cover page, dated a few weeks after quarter-end. The Q2 2025 net loss uses the restated figure. Q3 2026 will be added after the Nov 10 report.
